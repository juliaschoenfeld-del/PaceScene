Privacy notice

Launch MVP draft — not legal advice. This page should be reviewed for the territories where PaceScene launches.

What the app stores

Saved rehearsals and readiness ratings are stored locally in your browser in this MVP. PaceScene does not require an account.

AI generation

When you create a rehearsal, the answers in the creation form are sent to PaceScene's server function and then to the configured AI provider to generate the rehearsal. The API key is kept server-side and is never exposed in your browser.

What to avoid entering

Do not enter information you do not want processed for rehearsal generation. Avoid medical records, passwords, financial information, or identifying information about other people.

Analytics and cookies

This MVP does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Hosting infrastructure may keep operational logs.

Deletion

You can delete saved rehearsals from My Sessions. Because MVP data is stored locally, clearing site storage in your browser also removes it.
